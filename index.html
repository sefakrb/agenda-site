Ajanda

An Android agenda app by Sefa Karabaş.

Ajanda shows your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks in a single agenda and sends reminder notifications based on rules you set. Each person signs in with their own Google account and sees only their own data. The app is distributed directly as an APK to people the developer shares it with; it is not published on Google Play.

What the app does with Google data

  • Google Calendar: reads events to display them in the agenda and to schedule reminders; creates, edits and deletes events at the user's request.
  • Google Tasks: reads tasks; adds, completes, deletes tasks and changes their due date at the user's request.
  • Google Drive (app data folder only): stores an optional backup of the app's own settings and saved places/debts. The app cannot see any other file in Drive.
  • Email address: shown in the app to indicate which account is connected.

All data stays on each user's own device and in their own Google account. There is no server, no analytics and no third-party sharing, and the developer cannot see any user's data.
