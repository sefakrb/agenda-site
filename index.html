Ajanda

A personal Android agenda app by Sefa Karabaş.

Ajanda shows my Google Calendar events and Google Tasks in a single agenda and sends reminder notifications based on my own rules. It is a private, single-user app distributed as an APK; it is not published on Google Play.

What the app does with Google data

  • Google Calendar: reads events to display them in the agenda and to schedule reminders; creates, edits and deletes events at the user's request.
  • Google Tasks: reads tasks; adds, completes, deletes tasks and changes their due date at the user's request.
  • Google Drive (app data folder only): stores an optional backup of the app's own settings and saved places/debts. The app cannot see any other file in Drive.
  • Email address: shown in the app to indicate which account is connected.

All data stays on the user's device and in the user's own Google account. There is no server, no analytics and no third-party sharing.
